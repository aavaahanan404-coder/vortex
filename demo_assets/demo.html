The five-minute story

  1. Applicant: the portal tells you what to fix

    Open the applicant with a draft (cards, progress bar, 48-hour note).

    Say: "An institution uploads its documents. We check each one the moment it arrives and explain, in plain words, what to correct."

    • Press Check my application: the fire certificate is expired, a mandatory document is missing.
    • Open Values we read from this document: the form is pre-filled from the document.

    Point out: the applicant sees nothing about fraud checks, scores or other institutions.

    Open applicant (draft)

  2. A new applicant: upload, and a phone photo

    Say: "Most applicants will photograph papers. They drag four handles to the page corners; we flatten the page before reading it."

    • Open Applicant: new, fill the start form (values in the Files box above), then upload 01 to 09 from the sample folder. Each card turns green and shows the values read from it.
    • On Building plan approval choose 03_building_plan_PHONE_PHOTO.jpg: drag a corner handle, press Straighten, then Use this page. The certificate number and area are read from the photo.
    • Try 03_building_plan_BLURRY_PHOTO.jpg first if you want to show the instant "looks blurry" warning.
    • Press Check my application: ready to submit.

    Open applicant (new)

  3. Supervisor: the queue and the evidence

    Say: "The system never approves or rejects. It ranks cases for people, and shows exactly where it looked."

    • The queue is ordered by score. Open SYN-I0031 and choose the Fire safety certificate tab.
    • The date that was covered and replaced is boxed on the page; the finding is listed beside it.
    • Press Assign on SYN-I0001 in the queue; press Evidence report (PDF) on a case.

    Open supervisor

  4. Reviewer: decides blind

    Say: "Two reviewers look independently. They see the evidence but not the machine's score, so the machine cannot steer them."

    • Open the case SYN-I0031: the blue "Blind review" banner, no score anywhere.
    • Record a decision. It stays blind until the colleague decides; a third reviewer breaks a tie.

    Open reviewer (dev) Open reviewer (chi)

  5. Admin: change the rules by uploading a PDF

    Say: "When the regulator revises a norm, an administrator uploads the table. They see every change, up or down, and its effect on cases already received, before anything applies."

    • Rules and norms: Download template, then upload rule_update_example.pdf (also downloadable below).
    • Read the preview (arrows, rejected rows, effect on cases); tick the confirmation (it switches a rule off) and Apply.
    • Press Restore shipped rules, then Verify audit trail: every step is chained and tamper-evident.
    • Last, upload handbook_prose_example.pdf: it is refused with a plain explanation (prose handbooks are the planned AI step).

    Open admin Download example rule PDF

Be upfront

  • All data is synthetic and the registries are mock; the norms (staff ratios, areas) are illustrative, not the handbook's.
  • Sign-in here is by fixed token; a deployment uses the organisation's single sign-on.
  • Not built yet: the detector for copy-and-paste forgery inside a scan, and reading a prose handbook with AI (the rule PDF must be a table).
  • Photo straightening was tested on simulated photos, not yet on a pile of real phone photos.

If something is off

Quick fixes
  • Status stays "starting": the first start prepares the data for about a minute. Wait for "ready".
  • "Not reachable": the API window was closed. Close both demo windows and run start_demo.bat again.
  • To reset the demo to its first state: close both windows, delete the data_demo folder, start again.
  • Reviewer cases empty: use reviewer dev or chi for SYN-I0031, chi or ben for SYN-I0010.
