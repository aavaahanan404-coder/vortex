## What is and is not separable

- Strongly separable: sigma >= 1.25 vs sigma <= 0.5, with orders of magnitude of margin.
- Borderline: sigma 0.75 (VoL ~124..271) and sigma 1.0 (~52..127) touch each other across documents of different types. A single global threshold separates them on corpus_v2 only by a ~19-unit gap; per-type thresholds are needed to separate the held-out corpus. Types with one train source (eoa) are weak evidence.
- Not yet validated against OCR accuracy; the sigma-1.0 vs sigma-0.75 boundary is a policy call.
